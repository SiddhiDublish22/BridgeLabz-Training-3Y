Examples of :hover, :focus, :active, and :nth-child()

:hover

Hover on this link

The :hover pseudo-class applies styles when the mouse is over an element.

:focus

The :focus pseudo-class is applied when an element is focused (clicked or tabbed).

:active

The :active pseudo-class applies only while the element is being clicked.
